2. Configure Zendesk:

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Zendesk Ticket Created
         ↓
  Webhook → /webhook/ticket-created
         ↓
  Extract ticket data
         ↓
  Generate embedding
         ↓
  Search KB (Phase 1: manual)
         ↓
  Fallback to chat (Phase 2: if needed)
         ↓
  Generate reply (OpenAI)
         ↓
  Post to Zendesk
         ↓
  ✅ Reply appears in ticket!
